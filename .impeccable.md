## Design Context

### Users

The primary user is a technically fluent investor or research operator reviewing a 5–20 symbol US
technology watchlist. They use the product to understand what changed, assess evidence quality,
follow an AI research run, evaluate a paper portfolio, and audit every conclusion back to provider,
timestamp, ToolCall, and raw lineage. The product supports research and Paper Trading only.

### Brand Personality

Rigorous, calm, and audit-ready. The interface should create confidence through explicit provenance,
clear state, and restrained hierarchy—not through trading hype, gamification, or false certainty.

### Aesthetic Direction

A dense but readable research terminal: dark, ink-tinted surfaces; compact editorial typography;
tabular numerals; sharp separators; and a warm signal accent reserved for decisions and attention.
Avoid neon cyberpunk, generic card dashboards, brokerage-style buy/sell excitement, glassmorphism,
decorative charts, and color-only signals.

### Design Principles

1. Put decision context before decoration: market regime, freshness, provider health, and policy pins
   must stay near the conclusion they qualify.
2. Preserve audit trails in the information architecture: a user can move from summary to report,
   Claim, Evidence, ToolCall, provider, and timestamp without losing context.
3. Encode state redundantly with text, shape, and semantics; meet keyboard, visible-focus,
   reduced-motion, and non-color-only requirements.
4. Adapt density by context: desktop supports scanning and comparison; mobile preserves every critical
   action and fact through deliberate stacking rather than hiding functionality.
5. Treat uncertainty honestly: Empty, Stale, Provider-Degraded, Failure, and ABSTAIN are first-class
   product states, never disguised as success or zero values.
